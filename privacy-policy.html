Privacy Policy for Second Look

Effective date: October 22, 2026 Last updated: October 22, 2026

Second Look ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Second Look app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Second Look iOS app and any related services.

If anything here is unclear, contact us at thesecondlookai@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Second Look is operated by Siddhant Bajaj, based in New York, USA.

2. Information we collect

Account information. You sign in with Sign in with Apple or with your email address. With Apple, we receive the email address Apple provides, which may be Apple's private relay address. With email, we send a 6-digit code to the address you enter, and your account is created only once you enter that code. We don't accept sign-ups from known disposable email services. Either way, we store the email address with your account. We use it for account access and important messages about your account, and it is deleted when you delete your account. We do not send marketing emails without your separate consent. Some accounts created earlier also have a password.

Onboarding answers. When you start using the app, we ask three short questions: whether you share outfit photos with friends, whether you wish for a second look before leaving, and where you dress up for most. Your answers are stored with your account, used to choose your default occasion and to understand, in aggregate, how people use Second Look, and are deleted when you delete your account. They are never sent to our AI provider.

Your outfit photos and related data. When you take or select a photo to be analyzed:

  • The photo is sent from your device to our server, then to our AI provider for analysis. It may be sent to our AI provider more than once during a single analysis. It is never stored on our servers or by our AI provider once that analysis is complete.
  • If you ask how the same outfit reads for another occasion, or answer a quick question about a color during a scan, your phone sends the same photo again for that analysis. It is handled the same way and is never stored on our servers.
  • A structured, text-only description of the outfit in the photo (garment types, colors, general fit) is generated, and the feedback we show you is generated from it. Both are saved to your account so we can display your results, and, for subscribers, let you revisit past reads and see how the same outfit reads for other occasions. Neither includes information that identifies you personally, such as your face or body.
  • When you complete a scan, your phone keeps a small thumbnail and a resized copy of the photo on your device only, for your recent scans, so History can show the outfit and so the photo can be sent again for another occasion's read. They are never uploaded on their own and are not accessible to us. They are removed when you delete the scan from History, when it drops out of your 50 most recent scans, when you delete your account, or when you delete the app.
  • If you rate a fix, your rating and any written reason you add are stored on our servers, tied to your account, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • If you type where you are headed, that phrase is matched to an occasion and may appear briefly in our server logs. It is not saved to your account. Your recent occasions are kept on your phone so you can pick them again; they are cleared when you log out or delete your account.
  • We do not perform facial recognition, extract facial geometry, or create any biometric identifier from your photo.

Usage information. We record when scans happen, along with standard technical information such as IP address, device identifiers, and basic request logs, so we can enforce free-tier limits, prevent abuse, and operate the service reliably. This does not include the content of your photos.

Subscription information. If you subscribe, purchase and billing information is handled by Apple and our subscription management provider, RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of your subscription status, not your payment details — we never see or store your card information. RevenueCat separately collects a device identifier and records of subscription events (such as purchases and renewals) to manage this.

Age information. We ask for your date of birth to confirm you meet our minimum age requirement. We store only the resulting yes/no result, not the date of birth itself.

We do not use advertising networks or general-purpose analytics or tracking SDKs. The only third-party data collection beyond what's described above is RevenueCat's subscription-management data, noted above.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Analyze your outfit and generate feedback
  • Enforce free-tier scan limits and prevent abuse
  • Provide subscription features, including alternative-target reads and scan history
  • Maintain and improve the reliability and security of the app
  • Communicate with you about your account, if you've provided contact information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your photos or the descriptions and feedback generated from them for advertising.

4. How AI is used in this app — please read this section

Second Look's core feature works by sending your photo to a third-party artificial intelligence provider (currently Anthropic) for analysis. This is an automated process — no human reviews your photo as part of the normal operation of the app.

A few things worth understanding about this:

  • The feedback you receive is generated by an AI model based on patterns it has learned, not by a professional stylist. It is offered for informational and entertainment purposes and should not be treated as expert advice.
  • AI-generated feedback can be wrong, inconsistent, or miss things a human would notice. Use your own judgment.
  • Your photo is transmitted to our AI provider to generate this analysis. It is not used to train their models. Depending on the account arrangement in place with that provider, it may be briefly retained for a limited period to support safety and abuse-monitoring purposes, consistent with their standard data-handling policies, before being deleted — it is not kept for training or any purpose beyond that.
  • The AI is instructed not to comment on your body, face, or physical characteristics — only on clothing. If you ever see output that violates this, please report it to thesecondlookai@gmail.com.

5. Camera and Photo Library permissions

The app will ask your permission before accessing:

  • Camera — to let you take a photo of your outfit directly in the app.
  • Photo Library — to let you choose an existing photo instead of taking a new one.

You can decline either permission individually and still use the other to provide a photo. If you decline both, the app's core scan feature will not work, since it depends on having a photo one way or another — no other functionality is affected. You can change these permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings app, under Second Look.

We do not request access to your microphone, location, contacts, or any other device data.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to operate the app:

  • Anthropic (our AI provider) — receives your photo and related data to generate your analysis, as described above.
  • Supabase (our database and backend provider) — stores your account data, scan descriptions and feedback, and usage records, protected so that only you can access your own data.
  • Our email provider receives your email address only to send you sign-in codes, if you sign in with email.
  • Apple and RevenueCat — handle purchases, subscriptions, and billing for the app. RevenueCat receives a device identifier and subscription event data to do this.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not sell personal information.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Second Look, our users, or others.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your account data — the stored outfit descriptions, generated feedback, and usage records — is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, this data is permanently deleted, including from routine backups within 30 days, and including any cached descriptions used to support features like alternative-target reads.

Photos are never retained in the first place, so there is nothing to delete on that front — deletion happens automatically the moment analysis completes.

If Second Look is ever acquired, merged, or otherwise transferred, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We'll make reasonable efforts to notify you if this happens and if it materially changes how your data is handled.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. This includes restricting access so that each account's data is only ever accessible to that account, encrypting data in transit, limiting administrative access to backend systems, and never retaining the photos you submit for analysis. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Age requirement and children's privacy

Second Look is intended for users 16 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger than our minimum age requirement. If you believe a user under this age has provided us information, contact us at thesecondlookai@gmail.com so we can address it.

10. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app
  • Decline camera or photo library access (with the limitations noted above)
  • Contact us with questions or requests about your data at thesecondlookai@gmail.com

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (for example, the right to request a copy of your data, or to object to certain processing). Contact us to exercise these rights.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request its deletion, and request correction of inaccurate information, under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Contact us at thesecondlookai@gmail.com to make a request.

Second Look is operated from New York, USA. At this time, the app is only available in the United States, and your information is processed in the United States. If this changes, we will update this policy to reflect how information is handled for users in other regions.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and show a notice in the app before the changes take effect.
